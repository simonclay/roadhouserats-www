Meet

The Roadhouse Rats Band

Born in the back room of The Dashwood Roadhouse and raised on a diet of Marshall stacks and late-night lock-ins, Roadhouse Rats are the house band the Dashwood deserves. Five seasoned musicians, one shared mission: to play the classic rock anthems you know and love with the raw energy they were always meant to have.

Every last Saturday of the month, the Dashwood transforms. The lights drop, the amps go up, and the boys tear through a setlist that spans Deep Purple and Led Zeppelin to Bon Jovi, Queen and beyond. Just five people who genuinely love this music, playing it the way it deserves to be played.

Whether you’re a regular at the bar or stumbling in off the street for the first time, a Roadhouse Rats night is one you won’t forget in a hurry.
