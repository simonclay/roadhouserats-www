Privacy and Cookies

This page explains what information the Roadhouse Rats website collects, what we do with it, and how cookies are used. We’re a small band, so we keep it simple: we only collect what we need to answer your enquiry.

Who we are

Roadhouse Rats is a classic rock covers band based at The Dashwood Roadhouse, Piddington. If you have any questions about this page or your information, email us at [email address].

Booking enquiries

If you use the booking form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number (if you give it), the type of event, the date and times, and anything you tell us about the event.

We use these details only to reply to you and arrange the gig. We don’t add you to a mailing list, and we never sell or share your details with anyone else.

The form is run by Web3Forms, a service that passes your enquiry on to us by email. It handles your details on our behalf and may process them outside the UK. You can read Web3Forms’ privacy policy.

We delete enquiries once we no longer need them, and normally within 12 months, unless you’ve booked us and we need to keep a record of the booking.

Cookies

Cookies are small files a website saves on your device. This website doesn’t set any cookies of its own, and it doesn’t use analytics or advertising trackers, so you won’t see a cookie banner.

Videos

Our live videos are hosted on YouTube. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play on a video. When you do, the video plays from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced service (youtube-nocookie.com), but YouTube may still store some information on your device and receive your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. For help, visit aboutcookies.org.

Website hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub records the IP address of visitors for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Just email us at the address above.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
